### About Me

I'm a Computer Science & Engineering student who likes turning ambitious ideas into working systems, usually under a hackathon deadline. My projects tend to mix things that don't obviously belong together — bio-inspired algorithms with disaster logistics, OCR with sustainability tracking, blockchain with real-time dashboards. I care about clean architecture and secure-by-default design as much as I care about shipping something that actually runs.

### Currently

- Building multi-agent AI systems with fault-tolerant, multi-LLM pipelines
- Exploring zero-trust security patterns and on-chain verification with Solidity
- Sharpening DSA, DBMS, OS & Computer Networks fundamentals
- Exploring Claude Code, Cursor AI & Windsurf AI in my dev workflow
